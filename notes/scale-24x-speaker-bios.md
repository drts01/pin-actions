## 1. Executive / Professional

Use for the main schedule and the conference app.

Carlos Meza is a Senior Software Engineer at UnitedHealthcare, where he works on CI/CD, platform automation,
and developer experience on AWS.
He found his way into tech through Linux user groups, local meetups, and SCaLE.
His ten-plus years include engineering roles at Verizon, Everbridge, and The Walt Disney Studios.
At Disney, he designed and built a Policy-as-Code service that enforced cloud compliance automatically.
He chaired DevOpsDays LA, served on the Interop NOC team, and now leads a team on the SCaLE A/V crew.
At SCaLE 14x, he explained how DNSSEC proves a DNS answer is authentic.
At SCaLE 24x, his sixth time on a SCaLE stage, he asks the same of CI:
is the code your pipeline runs the code you reviewed?

GitHub: [drts01](https://github.com/drts01) · LinkedIn: [cmeza99](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cmeza99)

## 3. Lighthearted / Engaging

Use for the speaker page, a meetup post, or an informal CFP "about you" field.

Carlos Meza automates anything he has to do twice.
He calls it laziness.
Everyone else calls it platform engineering.

His route into tech ran through Linux user group meetings, local meetups, and SCaLE hallways.
He kept asking questions until he was the one answering them.

His toolchain is short: GNU/Linux, Python, and git.
At The Walt Disney Studios, he used it to build a Policy-as-Code service,
so cloud compliance became something the platform enforced instead of a meeting.
Today he is a Senior Software Engineer at UnitedHealthcare, automating CI/CD and developer experience on AWS.

He's also an avid board gamer, the kind who reads the whole rulebook before the first turn.
He reads CI workflows the same way.
Most teams pin their actions, while the container image three lines down still floats on a tag.
When he had to pin a pile of workflows, he wrote a tool to do it for him.
The list of things to pin turned out longer than the advice.

His time on the Interop NOC team taught him to trust hotel Wi-Fi about as much as a mutable tag.
He once chaired DevOpsDays LA and now leads a team on the SCaLE A/V crew.
If the mic works today, thank his team.
He pushes code as [drts01](https://github.com/drts01) on GitHub;
find him on LinkedIn as [cmeza99](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cmeza99).
