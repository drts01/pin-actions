## 1. Executive / Professional

Use for the CFP bio field, the main schedule, and the conference app.

Carlos Meza is a Senior Software Engineer at UnitedHealthcare, where he works on CI/CD, platform automation,
and developer experience on AWS.
He found his way into tech through Linux user groups, local meetups, and SCaLE.
His ten-plus years include engineering roles at Verizon, Everbridge, and The Walt Disney Studios.
At Disney, he designed and built a Policy-as-Code service that enforced cloud compliance automatically.
He chaired DevOpsDays LA, served on the Interop NOC team, and now leads a team on the SCaLE A/V crew.
He has spoken at SCaLE five times since SCaLE 14x.

GitHub: [drts01](https://github.com/drts01) · LinkedIn: [cmeza99](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cmeza99)

## Talk Hooks

Append one hook to a variant, before the handles line.
Use the formal hook with Variants 1 and 2, and the playful hook with Variant 3.
Variant 3 plus the playful pinning hook is about 1,085 characters;
trim it before using it in the 1000-character CFP bio field.

### Incomplete Pin References in CI

- **Formal:** At SCaLE 14x, he explained how DNSSEC proves a DNS answer is authentic.
  This talk asks the same of CI: is the code your pipeline runs the code you reviewed?
- **Playful:** Most teams pin their actions, while the container image three lines down still floats on a tag.
  When he had to pin a pile of workflows, he wrote a tool to do it for him.
  The list of things to pin turned out longer than the advice.

### dotfiles: The Missing Manual

- **Formal:**
  He has kept his dotfiles in a public Git repository since 2019
  and treats `$HOME` like a small production system: versioned, tested, and profiled.
- **Playful:** He has kept his dotfiles in Git since 2019.
  He used to spend months tuning a vimrc; now he writes tests for his home directory.
